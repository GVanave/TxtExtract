## Privacy plan (read before related work)

Kassenbon will be opened to invited guests (closed beta, "Option A"). The checklist is in
`docs/datenschutz-plan.md`. Before any work on users, sign-in, invitations, stored fields, logging, Gemini
configuration or hosting regions, read that plan, apply the relevant items, and tick them off there. No one
besides the owner may get access (e.g. via `ALLOWED_EMAILS`) until every "Pflicht vor dem ersten Gast" item is done.
